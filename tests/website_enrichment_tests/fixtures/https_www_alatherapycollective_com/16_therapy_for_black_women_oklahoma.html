For Black and African American Women

Therapy for Black Women in Oklahoma

Somewhere you do not have to hold it all together.

Schedule a Consultation Verify Your Benefits

You are the one everyone leans on. This is where you get to set that down.

Black and African American women get handed a particular kind of weight. Be strong. Hold the family, the job, the people who count on you. Keep it together when nobody thinks to ask how you are doing. Carrying that with nowhere to set it down is not strength. It is exhaustion, and it is what most people are actually walking in with.

Demetria Bonds is a Black woman and a social worker who works with Black adults and couples across Oklahoma. What people bring her is racial stress, family expectations, burnout, grief, relationship challenges, and the exhaustion that comes from constantly adapting to spaces that were never designed with you in mind. She also works with LGBTQIA+ clients and with Black adults and couples more broadly. She has heard all of it before, which means you are not starting from scratch.

Nobody here needs the backstory before they can take you seriously.

What We Help With

What you have been holding.

The reasons Black and African American women reach out here are rarely one thing. Usually it is several at once, and most of them have been running in the background for years.

  • Racial stress, and the cost of being the only one in the room
  • Family expectations, and the roles that got assigned before you could argue
  • Grief and loss, including the losses you were expected to work through on schedule
  • Burnout and anxiety, and the kind of tired that sleep does not fix
  • Relationships, boundaries, and being everyone's safe place but your own
  • Identity and self worth, and who you are when nobody needs anything from you
  • Motherhood, family, and the labor nobody names
  • The exhaustion of constantly adapting to spaces that were never designed with you in mind
How She Works

Go back and fetch it.

Demetria's practice runs on Sankofa. The word means to go back and fetch it, and the bird is drawn looking backward while its feet still point forward. That is the whole idea. You do not heal by pretending the past did not happen. You go back, you look at it honestly, you take what is worth carrying, and you leave the rest where it is. Every part of your story counts, including the parts you would rather skip.

What that looks like in a session is unglamorous, and it is much the same as what to expect anywhere in this practice. You talk. She listens. There is no test to pass and no correct way to describe what is wrong, and you do not need a tidy reason for being there. From there it gets practical. She works with cognitive behavioral therapy, motivational interviewing, interpersonal process, solution focused work, and strengths based and trauma informed approaches, and she picks based on you instead of running everyone through the same sequence. Some weeks are about what happened. Some weeks are about Tuesday.

Grief

The losses nobody gave you time for.

Demetria has hospice and palliative care experience behind her, and it shapes how she works with grief, loss, and caregiver stress. This is not a subject she has to be eased into.

And grief here is not only funerals. It is the caregiving that ate years of your life. It is the anticipatory kind, where you are grieving someone who is still in the room. It is divorce, a diagnosis, leaving a church, an empty house, a version of your future you had already decorated. All of it counts, and none of it comes with a timeline you are supposed to be hitting.

Relationships

You can bring the relationship too.

Demetria does couples work, so if what is wearing you down is happening inside a relationship, that is on the table. Communication that has broken down. Trust that got damaged. The mental load that somehow all landed on you. The same argument on a loop. She works with married, dating, and partnered clients, and with every configuration including non monogamous and polyamorous relationships. She also runs the premarital counseling package. More on the couples therapy page.

Where

Oklahoma, all of it.

Sessions are online across the whole state, and in person at the Oklahoma City office on Greenbriar Parkway. Whichever one you will actually keep doing is the right one.

Most of this practice already runs online, because most of this state is not Oklahoma City. If the nearest Black or African American therapist who takes your plan is two towns over and booked solid, telehealth is not the consolation prize. It is the only version of the math that works. More on online therapy in Oklahoma.

Questions Women Ask

Before you reach out.

Do I have to be Black to book here?

No. This page is written for Black and African American women who would rather not spend the first month explaining themselves, or shrinking to fit the room. Anyone is welcome to reach out.

My family does not believe in therapy. Is that going to be a whole thing?

Not here. Plenty of people start without telling anyone, and nobody is going to ask you to pick a side between getting help and the people you love. What you say in session is yours.

Do you take insurance?

Demetria is in network with Aetna, Ambetter, BCBS, FEP BCBS, Cigna and Evernorth, Dean Health Plan, Healthcare Highways, Medica, Mending, and WebTPA. Reach out with your plan details and we will verify your benefits before your first session.

What if I cannot afford it?

Tell us. A sliding scale is available, and we would rather work the number out with you than have cost be the reason you never started.

Is everything virtual, or can I come in?

Both. Demetria sees clients online across Oklahoma and in person at the Oklahoma City office on Greenbriar Parkway. Pick whichever one you will actually keep doing.

Can I bring my partner?

Yes. Demetria does couples work alongside individual therapy, for married, dating, and partnered clients, and for every configuration including non monogamous and polyamorous relationships.

Will I be judged for finally asking for help?

No. Needing support does not make you weak or behind. It makes you human, and this is a place to be one.

How do I start?

Reach out through our contact form. We will get back to you and set up a time to talk.
