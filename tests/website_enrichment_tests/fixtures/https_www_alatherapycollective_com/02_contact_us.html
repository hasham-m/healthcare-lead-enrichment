Questions

Contact and scheduling FAQ

How do I get started with therapy at Ala Therapy Collective?

Click Get Started and complete our secure form. Tell us what you are looking for, pick a preferred clinician if you have one, and add your insurance details if you are using benefits. We follow up by email to schedule your consultation. If you already know who you want to see, you can request a consultation time directly in the client portal instead.

How do I verify my insurance benefits?

Select insurance as your payment method in the Get Started form and upload a photo of the front and back of your card. We contact your plan, confirm your mental health coverage, and let you know what we find, including any copay or deductible details, before your first session.

What insurance plans does Ala Therapy Collective accept?

We accept Aetna, Ambetter, Anthem, BCBS, FEP BCBS, Carelon, Cigna, Dean Health Plan, Empac, Evernorth, GEHA, Harvard Pilgrim, Healthchoice, Healthcare Highways, Mass General Brigham, Medica, Medicare, Optum, Taro Health, TriCare East/Humana, TriWest, Tufts, UMR, UHC, and WebTPA. Private pay and sliding scale options are also available.

Do you offer online therapy outside Oklahoma?

Yes. Ala Therapy Collective is based in Oklahoma and offers online therapy with clinicians licensed in Oklahoma, California, Hawaii, Massachusetts, Texas, and Utah. Availability by state depends on each clinician's licensure, and we will match you accordingly when you reach out.

Do you provide immigration psychological evaluations?

Yes. We provide immigration psychological evaluations in Oklahoma, California, Hawaii, Massachusetts, Texas, and Utah, including weekend appointments. Select the immigration evaluation option in the Get Started form or visit our immigration evaluations page to learn more.

I am a current client. How do I reschedule or cancel?

Sign in to the client portal to reschedule, cancel, or request a new appointment time. You can also send your clinician a secure message there or reach the office at hello@alatherapycollective.com.
